# The void map

## The rule

`the-fold-theorems.md` read twelve theorems and asked which premises the Fold already has. This note reads
the other side: ten words physics cannot do without, and asks which of them the Fold has an exact object
for. The answer is the same in every row, and it is not "none":

> the nearest object is exact; the word is the absence.

That is the mirror of the ledger. There, the missing premise was the finding; here, the missing structure
is. A word is not void because the Fold has nothing near it. It is void because the nearest object is a
different kind of thing, and the difference can be named.

Every nearest object in the second column is exact in the Fold's own terms. That is why the table is not
a list of failures: the Fold reaches the object and stops at the structure that would make it physical.

## Ten words, six hosts

The first finding is compression. The ten missing structures are not ten independent mysteries. They are
six:

| host | words it would carry | what it adds |
|---|---|---|
| a measure | probability, entropy, vacuum | a `σ`-algebra, a normalized measure, a lowest-energy state |
| a flow | time, energy | a one-parameter group that is not an involution, and an action |
| a bath | temperature | a reservoir and a coupling that lets a ratio become a law |
| a cut | measurement | an interaction and a rule that turns a section into a record |
| a bundle | charge, field | a base, a connection, and a kinetic term |
| an inertia | mass | a force law and a unit |

The first four are the adjunctions of `the-fold-agi.md`, with the observer's cut named as the host. The
last two are not, and that is the finding: the four-stage programme is incomplete. A gauge theory is not
a measure, a flow, a bath, or an observer. It is a bundle over a base, and the Fold has the fiber and
the form but not the base. Mass is not a flow either. It is a coupling, and the Fold has the scale but
not the inertia.

So the ten words reduce to six hosts, and the AGI note's four adjunctions cover four of them. The void
map is the argument for a fifth and sixth adjunction: adjoin a bundle, then an inertia.

## What is exact in each row

The table would be too easy if "nearest object" meant "vague resemblance". Each row has a specific exact
residue, and the residue is what a later note can use:

**Mass.** The zero at a scale is exact, and the height law gives `M` exponent `p = 1`. The Fold can say
what a mass ratio is: `(h₁/h₂)(f₁/f₂)`, with only the first factor a height. It cannot say what inertia
is, because inertia is a response to a force and the Fold has no force. The blocked statement is not the
mass reading; it is `F = ma`.

**Energy.** `E ↦ −E` is an exact involution, and the particle-hole reading is exact at the level of the
sign. The homothety generator `k ∂_k` is exact too. What is missing is the conserved charge: Noether
needs an action, and the ledger has already recorded that the Fold's only conserved quantity is the
degree. Energy is the degree's physical name, and the name is the borrow.

**Time.** The twist phase is exact, and the calendar is exact as a quotient of periods: a clock is a
period, and a calendar is a set of periods identified. What is missing is the arrow. The fold is an
involution, `σ² = 1`; a flow is a one-parameter group with an arrow, and `σ` does not supply the arrow.
The horizon clock `T ∝ 1/k` is a reading of the period, not a derivation of evolution.

**Temperature.** The ratio `T ∝ 1/k` is exact as a ratio, and `g_k` preserves it. What is missing is the
bath: a temperature is a property of a state in equilibrium with a reservoir, and the Fold has neither
the state nor the reservoir. `μ = τ/p*` is universal for the same reason `T_c/T_h` is: both are ratios
of two energies. That is a fact about ratios, not a theorem about ensembles.

**Entropy.** The area `A` and the mode count are exact; the exponent is the one the horizon notes
compute. What is missing is the measure and the normalization. `S = A/4` is a statement in Planck units,
and the `4` is not in the Fold. The area law's shape transfers; its coefficient does not.

**Charge.** The degree is exact: `∮ dk/k = 2πi`, and `Res d log` reads multiplicity. This is the least
void row. The Fold has the integer, and the integer is the charge. What is missing is the field the
charge sources: a gauge connection, a base, and a coupling. Gauss's law is blocked, not charge
quantization.

**Field.** The `1`-form `ω = dk/k` is exact, and `w₁` is an exact obstruction to patching fiber
involutions over a base. A physical field is a section of a bundle with a connection and a kinetic term;
the Fold has the fiber and the form, not the base or the term. Maxwell and Yang–Mills are blocked at the
base, not at the form.

**Probability.** The quotient `z(1 − z)` is exact, and the counting measure on `ℤ/n` is exact. What is
missing is the normalized measure on a space of states and the independence of trials. The Born rule is
blocked at the measure, not at the quotient. This is the row where a finite host is closest: `ℤ/n` with
`1/n` is a probability space, and the missing piece is the map from states to it.

**Vacuum.** The zero object is exact, and the empty set is exact. Neither is a vacuum: a vacuum is the
lowest-energy state of a Hamiltonian, and the Fold has no Hamiltonian. The cosmological constant is
blocked at the energy, not at the emptiness. "Nothing" in the Fold is a zero object, not a state.

**Measurement.** The cut and the window are exact; the observer note computes the resolution. What is
missing is the interaction and the rule. A measurement is a coupling that leaves a record, and a record
is a stable section. The Fold has the section and not the coupling. The measurement problem is blocked
at the interaction, not at the cut.

## The order of the hosts

The six hosts are not independent, and the order is forced by what each one needs:

```
measure → flow → bath → cut
            ↘ bundle → charge
measure + flow → inertia → mass
```

A flow needs a measure to have a state to move. A bath needs a flow to have a time. A cut needs a bath to
have a record. A bundle needs a base, and the base is a spacetime the Fold does not have; charge then
needs the bundle. Inertia needs a flow and a measure: a force is a change of momentum, and momentum needs
a time and a state. So the order is not a preference. It is the dependency graph, and it says the four
adjunctions of the AGI note are the first four nodes, not the whole graph.

The one node that needs more than the graph is mass. It needs a flow and a measure, but it also needs a
unit. The Fold's exact quantities are ratios; a mass in kilograms needs a scale to compare to, and the
scale is a host. That is why mass is the last node, not the first.

## The test

The void map is falsifiable in the same way the ledger was. A row moves when a note supplies the missing
structure, and the move is visible in the table:

- **charge** moves first. The degree is already exact; a note that writes a bundle over a base and a
  coupling would move charge from "field missing" to the ledger. The field row moves with it.
- **probability** moves second. `ℤ/n` with `1/n` is a finite probability space; a note that names the
  map from states to it would move the Born rule from "measure missing" to "reformulation".
- **mass** moves last. It needs a unit, and a unit is a comparison to a host, not a theorem.

If no row moves, the map is a description, not a test. If a row moves without a new structure, the row
was mislabelled, and the map is the place to say so.

## The zones of this note

- the nearest objects and the numbers are exact where they are stated; the table's second column is the
  exact residue of each word, and the blocked statements are named, not proved;
- the reduction of ten words to six hosts is a reformulation: it regroups the absences, it does not
  supply them;
- the order of the hosts is a reading of the dependency graph, not a theorem;
- "the nearest object is exact; the word is the absence" is the note's one sentence, and it is a
  definition of the void, not a claim about the world.

The one sentence: the Fold reaches the object and stops at the structure, and the structure has six
names.
