One row is the whole answer. k = −1 is the only scaling that also appears in the fold column: it preserves every distance and reverses every direction. That is the point reflection — the member the paper calls the fold. For any other k, length is lost and direction is kept; for any reflection, direction is flipped and length is kept. Angles and the cross-ratio survive both, and they are the only nontrivial things that do.
