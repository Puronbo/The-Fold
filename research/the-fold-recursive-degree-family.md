# Recursive degree families and uniform composition-excess sign

## G-EP208 — A recursively growing family with a uniform sign theorem

Let
\[
m_k=3^k,\qquad k\ge1,
\]
and consider the pairwise-coprime degree pair \((2,m_k)\).

For a prime power field size \(Q\), recall
\[
E_Q(n)=1-R_Q(n),\qquad
H_Q(a,b)=E_Q(ab)-E_Q(a)E_Q(b).
\]

Since \(2\) and \(3\) are the only distinct prime divisors of \(2\cdot3^k\), the exact formulas are
\[
E_Q(2)=Q^{-1},\qquad
E_Q(3^k)=Q^{-2m_k/3},
\]
and
\[
E_Q(2m_k)=Q^{-m_k}+Q^{-4m_k/3}-Q^{-5m_k/3}.
\]
Therefore
\[
\boxed{
H_Q(2,3^k)=
Q^{-m_k}+Q^{-4m_k/3}-Q^{-5m_k/3}
-Q^{-(1+2m_k/3)}.
}
\]

### Theorem: sign transition at k=1

- For \(k=1\), \(m_1=3\), and
  \[
  H_Q(2,3)=Q^{-4}-Q^{-5}>0
  \]
  for every real \(Q\ge2\).
- For every \(k\ge2\), \(m_k\ge9\), and
  \[
  \boxed{H_Q(2,3^k)<0\quad\text{for every real }Q\ge2.}
  \]

### Proof for k >= 2

Set \(a=1+2m/3\), where \(m=3^k\). Since \(m\ge9\), the exponent gaps satisfy
\[
m-a=m/3-1\ge2,
\qquad
4m/3-a=2m/3-1\ge5.
\]
Factor out the dominant negative term:
\[
H_Q(2,m)
=-Q^{-a}\left(
1-Q^{-(m/3-1)}-Q^{-(2m/3-1)}
+Q^{-(m-1)}
\right).
\]
For every real \(Q\ge2\), the bracket is bounded below by
\[
1-2^{-2}-2^{-5}>0,
\]
because the final term is nonnegative. Thus the bracket is strictly positive, proving the claimed negative sign uniformly for all real \(Q\ge2\).

### Why this connects recursion to spectral folding

The family is generated by the simple recurrence
\[
m_{k+1}=3m_k,\qquad m_1=3.
\]
Its members are pairwise coprime with 2, and their composition-excess spectra have an explicit closed form. The leading exponent changes with k, but the sign remains uniformly negative after the exceptional resonant base case k=1.

This is stronger than a finite-family threshold: it is an infinite-family sign theorem with the same domain \(Q\ge2\), proved from the exact spectrum.

### Boundary and scope

The theorem concerns the composition-excess sign \(H_Q(2,3^k)\), not the temporal second difference of the sequence in k. The sign of the temporal fold
\[
H_Q(2,3^{k+1})-2H_Q(2,3^k)+H_Q(2,3^{k-1})
\]
is a separate question and is not inferred from constant negativity. The sequence can stay negative while its discrete curvature changes sign.
