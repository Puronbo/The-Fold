Two creases at angle α compose to a rotation by 2α. The family closes only when α/π is rational: then the group is the dihedral group Dm of order 2m, and the twist is an m-th root of unity. 45° is the m = 4 case — the first where that root is purely imaginary, a bare quarter turn (k = i); m = 3 gives −0.5 + 0.866i, which still has a real part. At α = 0 the creases coincide (identity); separated parallel creases instead compose to a translation by twice their gap — the non-compact limit, datum at infinity.
