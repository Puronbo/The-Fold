# UF-019 — Hilbert function and associated graded algebra at the Fold vertex

Let R0=k[x,y]/(x²+y²,x²y²), over a field of characteristic zero, and let m=(x,y). Since y²=−x², the second relation is equivalent to x⁴=0:
\[
R_0\cong k[x,y]/(y^2+x^2,x^4).
\]

## Theorem UF-019.1

The m-adic filtration has Hilbert function
\[
\boxed{H_{R_0}(t)=1+2t+2t^2+2t^3+t^4.}
\]
The successive dimensions are (1,2,2,2,1), and m⁵=0 while m⁴≠0.

A homogeneous basis by degree is
\[
1;\quad x,y;\quad x^2,xy;\quad x^3,x^2y;\quad x^3y.
\]
Indeed y²=−x², xy²=−x³, y³=−x²y, and all degree-five monomials vanish using x⁴=0 and y²=−x².

The total length is 1+2+2+2+1=8, as required by the complete-intersection degree product 2·4.

## Consequences

1. Embedding dimension dim_k(m/m²)=2.
2. Loewy length is five: m⁵=0 and m⁴≠0.
3. The socle is one-dimensional, generated by x³y; this Artin complete intersection is Gorenstein.
4. The Hilbert function is symmetric, as expected for an Artin graded complete intersection.

This refines UF-018: the Jordan type [4,4] of each coordinate multiplication operator is not the whole local structure. The Hilbert function records the two-dimensional tangent space and one-dimensional top socle.

The script tools/fold_vertex_hilbert.py constructs the eight-dimensional quotient using the monomial basis and checks dimensions of powers of the maximal ideal by multiplication.

## Follow-up

Determine whether the Hilbert function, socle pairing, and simultaneous multiplication representation together classify this vertex algebra among nearby length-eight complete intersections. The Hilbert function alone is not a complete isomorphism invariant.
