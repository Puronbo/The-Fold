The apex is the datum and the rim is the range, so the body is the set of directions within θ reached out to R. Its shape is the aperture; its size is the range. Scaling k moves only the second: Ω is invariant, V ∝ k³, A ∝ k², V/A ∝ k. The fold sends the cone to its antipode — same aperture, opposite direction — and fixes it only at θ = 180°, where the body is a ball and there is no direction left to flip.
