Halving the segment between a point and its image is itself a homothety: same datum, factor (1+k)/2. Its determinant vanishes at exactly one value, k = −1 — and there the midpoint map is constant. Every midpoint is the datum, so the fold is the member of the family in which the datum is forced to be the midpoint. Two other values put a midpoint somewhere useful: k = ½ makes the image the midpoint of the datum and the point, k = 2 makes the point the midpoint of the datum and the image.
