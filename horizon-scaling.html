The two regimes draw parallel lines or crossing lines, and that is the whole difference. In the homothetic family r_H/R is the same at every k, so the horizon is either present for every member or for none — a label, not a stage. In a real collapse the mass is fixed, r_H = r_s is a flat line, and the body falls onto it at k* = r_s/R₀. Nothing obstructs the homothetic descent to zero; the collapse is stopped at k* > 0 by a horizon that the scaling table cannot see.
