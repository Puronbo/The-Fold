# UF-046 — Fourth-power extensions of the reduced quotient coordinates

## Setup and notation

Let k have characteristic two and
\[
C=k[x,z]/(x^4,z^2).
\]
Use the full automorphism substitution
\[
\begin{aligned}
X&=c+ax+bz+\alpha x^2+\cdots,\\
Z&=d_0+hx+iz+\phi x^2+\eta xz+\cdots.
\end{aligned}
\]
The full automorphism scheme has relations
\[
c^4=0,\qquad d_0^2=0,\qquad h^2=0,
\]
and the cotangent determinant \(ai-bh\) is invertible. On the reduced subgroup, \(c=d_0=h=0\), and the graded parameters \(a,i\) are units.

The distinction between \(d_0\), the constant coefficient of \(Z\), and \(i\), the coefficient of \(z\), is essential.

## Reduced quotient coordinates

On the reduced chart where \(a\) and \(i\) are invertible, normalize by the graded substitution
\[
g(x)=ax+bz,\qquad g(z)=iz.
\]
As derived in UF-045, the quotient coordinates of
\(Q=\mathbb G_a^2\rtimes B\) are
\[
F=\frac{\phi}{i},\qquad
R=\frac{\alpha}{a}+\frac{b\phi}{ai}+\frac{\eta}{i}.
\]

## Theorem — Fourth powers have canonical lifts

Let \(A\) be the coordinate ring of the full automorphism scheme on this chart, and let \(A_{\mathrm{red}}\) be its reduction. The nilradical is generated by \(c,d_0,h\). For every \(n\) in this nilradical,
\[
n^4=0.
\]
Indeed, in characteristic two the fourth-power map is additive, and each of the three generators has fourth power zero.

If \(f\in A_{\mathrm{red}}\) and \(\widetilde f_1,\widetilde f_2\in A\) are any two lifts, their difference is nilpotent, so
\[
\widetilde f_1^4-\widetilde f_2^4
=(\widetilde f_1-\widetilde f_2)^4=0.
\]
Thus the fourth power of a reduced function has a unique lift to A, independent of the choice of representative.

Applying this to F and R gives
\[
\boxed{\widetilde F=\frac{\phi^4}{i^4}}
\]
and
\[
\boxed{
\widetilde R=
\frac{\alpha^4}{a^4}
+\frac{b^4\phi^4}{a^4i^4}
+\frac{\eta^4}{i^4}.
}
\]
These are regular functions on the localization where \(a,i\) are units.

## Consequence for the second-Frobenius quotient morphism

The second relative Frobenius factors through the reduction of the full automorphism scheme. Therefore the pullbacks of the quotient coordinates on \(Q^{(2)}\) are precisely these canonical fourth-power lifts (together with the fourth powers of the graded coordinates). On this chart the quotient-kernel equations are
\[
a^4-1=0,\quad b^4=0,\quad i^4-1=0,
\quad \widetilde F=0,\quad \widetilde R=0.
\]
This is the local expression of the UF-043 candidate after pullback to the full scheme.

## Scope and caveats

1. The construction proves uniqueness of fourth-power lifts; it does not prove that the five equations alone present the full ambient coordinate ring or the minimal normal closure.
2. The displayed rational expressions are valid on the localization where \(a,i\) are invertible. The full automorphism scheme is defined on the larger determinant-open locus \(ai-bh\ne0\); a global presentation requires compatible charts or a denominator-free expression.
3. The existence of this Frobenius-kernel morphism still gives an upper bound for the scheme-theoretic normal closure. Equality with the minimal normal closure remains unproved.
